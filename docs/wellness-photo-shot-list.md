## Notes for the shoot

**Scent variants share a photo.** All 67 Whipped Shea Butter scents use the same tub image;
the scent is chosen from a picker on the product page. Same for the oils. Do **not** shoot
67 tubs.

**Scented Oil is worth 5 shots, not 1, if you have time.** The bottle sizes are visibly
different products at very different prices, and a customer choosing between them benefits
from seeing them:

| Size | Price |
|------|------:|
| 1/4 oz | $5.00 |
| Roll-on | $8.00 |
| 1/2 oz | $10.00 |
| 1 oz | $15.00 |
| 2 oz | $25.00 |

A single shot of the 1 oz bottle is enough to launch; the rest is an upgrade.

**Pre-optimize before upload.** The Media collection has no `imageSizes` — uploads store the
raw original with no automatic resizing. A 4 MB phone photo will be served to customers at
4 MB. Resize to ~1600px on the long edge and compress before uploading, or the product pages
will be slow.

**Consistency matters more than production value.** Same background, same lighting, same angle,
same crop across all 44. A shelf of mismatched photos reads as amateur far more than a shelf of
plain, consistent ones. A white or neutral sweep, diffuse daylight, and a fixed camera position
will beat a set of individually-pretty but inconsistent shots.

## How to publish a product once its photo exists

1. Payload admin → **Wellness Lifestyle** (or **Oils & Incense**).
2. The list view shows a **📷 Missing** marker on every product still waiting on a photo — that
   column is your worklist.
3. Open the product, upload the image to **Hero Image**, then tick **Publish Online**.
4. It appears on `/shop/health-and-beauty` immediately. Nothing is customer-visible until that
   box is ticked.

## Deliberately not in this list

- **Sea Moss** ($34,323/yr — your single biggest family). Refrigerated perishable; shipping it
  needs a cold-chain story that doesn't exist yet. Phase 2.
- **Bitters & Tonics** ($23,504/yr). SKU names like *AIH Blood Pressure*, *Firm & Flat Tonic*,
  and *Liver Health Capsules* make disease claims. Selling those in person is one thing;
  publishing an indexed product page titled "Blood Pressure" is FDA/FTC territory. Needs a
  renaming + disclaimer pass first. Phase 2.
